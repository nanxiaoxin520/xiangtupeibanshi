death in young people, and risk factors for suicide have continued to increase 
in recent years. Although interventions have been a primary focus of researchers 
and clinicians, research has also addressed the accurate assessment of risk for 
suicide. The Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory - 3 (MMPI-3), the most 
recent version of the MMPI family of instruments, is one of the most widely used 
psychological assessments in a multitude of settings. The current study examined 
the ability of the Suicidal/Death Ideation (SUI) scale on the MMPI-3 to classify

clinical interview associated with the interpersonal-psychological theory of 
suicide. Our results, if corroborated by future studies in higher-acuity 
settings, may lead to further interpretive statements at higher levels of 
elevation on the SUI scale.
